How Command is organized
Work is the execution record. Knowledge is the source for instructions and SOPs. The Prompt Library holds reusable preparation. CRM holds people, organizations, relationships, and opportunities. Email Desk holds drafts for review; completed communication history lives in CRM activities.
For recurring work: record the owner, next action, and when it matters in Work; keep the human-readable instructions in Living Docs; escalate sensitive or consequential decisions to the founder.
Operator loop: open assigned Work, handle due items, keep CRM and program state current, prepare communications without sending, maintain only documented consent, reconcile audit/fulfillment state, and escalate ambiguous or high-value decisions.
Weekly check: CRM hygiene, deadlines, follow-ups, draft cleanup, consent/suppression review, audit/fulfillment reconciliation, SOP maintenance, and a founder escalation summary.
